# 📊 Sales & Profit Analytics Dashboard

## Overview
An interactive **Power BI Sales & Profit Analytics Dashboard** designed to monitor commercial performance across years, quarters, product categories, visitor segments, and discount levels.

The dashboard contains multiple views, including an **Overview** and a detailed **Sales** analysis, with interactive filters for exploring the data.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Monitor core sales and profitability KPIs.
- Compare performance between **2025 and 2026**.
- Analyze sales and profit by category.
- Understand performance across visitor/customer segments.
- Track quarterly profit trends.
- Explore the effect of discounts and interactive filters.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Microsoft Power BI**
- Data modeling & relationships
- DAX measures
- Interactive slicers
- KPI cards
- Donut charts
- Column charts
- Line charts

## 📈 Key Insights

### 1. Sales increased from 2025 to 2026
Gross sales are approximately **1.60M in 2025** and **1.65M in 2026**, an increase of about **50K (3.1%)**.

### 2. Profit increased year over year
Displayed profit is approximately **1.08M in 2025** and **1.13M in 2026**, an increase of about **50K (4.6%)**.

### 3. Quarterly profit shows a downward trend
The quarterly profit visual starts around **581K in Q1**, remains around **555K in Q2**, moves to roughly **538K in Q3**, and falls to about **526K in Q4**.

### 4. Category performance is uneven
The category visual shows noticeable differences between categories, with the largest displayed category contributing roughly **922K** in net sales while other categories contribute smaller amounts.

### 5. Visitor segments contribute differently
The profit-by-visitor-segment visual shows a strong difference between segments. The largest displayed segment contributes roughly **1.22M**, compared with approximately **480K**, **273K**, and **22K** for the other displayed segments.

### 6. Discounts are a significant tracked factor
The dashboard reports approximately **229K** in discounts. This allows the relationship between promotional activity, cost, sales, and profit to be explored.

### 7. Filters reveal different business segments
When a category filter is applied in the recording, one filtered view shows approximately **1.05M net sales**, **884.2K profit**, **4K quantity**, **170K total cost**, and **88K discount amount**.
